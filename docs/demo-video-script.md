# DealMind: 90-Second Demo Video Script

**0:00 - 0:10 (Problem)**
*Visual: A rep scrolling endlessly through a generic CRM screen filled with text notes.*
**Voiceover:** Sales reps waste countless hours digging through old notes to prepare for meetings. Standard CRMs are static, and typical AI chatbots forget everything instantly.

**0:10 - 0:25 (ABC Motors Deal)**
*Visual: Click into DealMind dashboard, select "ABC Motors". Scroll through the 'Recent Activities' timeline highlighting a pricing objection and an AutoCorp mention.*
**Voiceover:** Enter DealMind. As sales interactions happen, DealMind silently extracts key facts—like pricing objections and competitor mentions—and stores them in a persistent AI memory bank powered by Hindsight. 

**0:25 - 0:40 (Ask DealMind to Prepare)**
*Visual: Click the "Prepare Meeting" button. Zoom in on the loading indicators (Retrieving memories...)*
**Voiceover:** Let's prepare for our next meeting. With one click, DealMind queries Hindsight for the exact semantic history of this deal, retrieving years of context in milliseconds. 

**0:40 - 0:55 (Hindsight Memories Retrieved & Recommendation)**
*Visual: The Meeting Brief renders. Highlight the Recommended Next Action and specific Competitor insights.*
**Voiceover:** The LLM receives this memory and generates a highly personalized, strategic meeting brief. It knows they want a discount and it knows how to position against AutoCorp.

**0:55 - 0:70 (Show why the recommendation was generated)**
*Visual: Click "Why this recommendation?". The raw Hindsight memory fragments expand.*
**Voiceover:** Most importantly, DealMind provides total transparency. Click "Why this recommendation?" to see the exact historical memories that drove the AI's advice. No hallucinations—just real deal intelligence. 

**0:70 - 0:90 (Learning Impact)**
*Visual: Open "Learning Demo" page. Show the Before/After comparison screen and Scorecard.*
**Voiceover:** Because it uses Hindsight, DealMind actually learns. The more you use it, the smarter it gets at anticipating objections and winning deals. This is DealMind—closing deals with perfect memory.
