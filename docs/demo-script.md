# DealMind: Hackathon Demo Script

## 1. Problem Statement (0:00 - 0:15)
"Sales representatives waste hours manually digging through old CRM notes, emails, and calls trying to remember specific customer concerns before critical meetings. Standard AI chatbots can't help because they forget everything the moment the chat window closes."

## 2. Introduce Deal History (0:15 - 0:30)
"Let's look at a realistic deal: ABC Motors. Over the past 40 days, we've had discovery calls, technical deep dives, and negotiations."
*(Navigate to Deals -> ABC Motors)*
"Notice the recent activities: They complained about pricing. They mentioned evaluating AutoCorp. And the procurement manager just asked for a 10% discount."

## 3. The DealMind AI Agent (0:30 - 0:45)
"Normally, a rep would have to memorize this. But behind the scenes, DealMind has been quietly persisting every one of these interactions into **Hindsight**, a powerful semantic vector memory system. Let's see what happens when the rep needs to prepare."
*(Click 'Prepare Meeting')*

## 4. Hindsight Memory Retrieval (0:45 - 1:00)
"DealMind instantly identifies the deal context, queries Hindsight's vector memory specifically for this deal's history, and retrieves the most relevant semantic matches."
*(Point to the animated status indicators: 'Retrieving Hindsight memories...' -> '6 relevant memories found')*

## 5. Personalized Recommendation & Transparency (1:00 - 1:20)
"The AI generates a highly personalized briefing. But more importantly..."
*(Click 'Why this recommendation? (Show)')*
"...DealMind guarantees transparency. It shows you the exact historical memories—pulled natively from Hindsight—that influenced its reasoning. It's not hallucinating; it's remembering."

## 6. The Learning Impact Dashboard (1:20 - 1:30)
*(Navigate to Learning Demo page)*
"As you can see, without memory, standard AI gives generic, useless advice. But powered by Hindsight, DealMind learns customer priorities, identifies recurring objections, and actually helps you close the deal."
